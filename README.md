# Uncertainty-Aware Surgical Navigation

A simulation-based research engineering project investigating how
perception uncertainty influences safety-critical motion planning in
minimally invasive surgical robotics.

The project develops and evaluates perception and planning strategies
under controlled visual degradation, localisation uncertainty and
occlusion. The system is designed to investigate whether task-aware
uncertainty can be used to guide perception and improve downstream
navigation safety.

## Research Question

Can task-aware uncertainty-driven perception improve the safety and
efficiency of motion planning in simulated minimally invasive surgical
environments compared with fixed-view and task-agnostic perception?

## Planned Comparison

Three perception strategies are evaluated:

1. Fixed-view perception
2. Generic uncertainty-aware active perception
3. Task-aware uncertainty-driven active perception

Performance will be evaluated using safety, perception, planning and
efficiency metrics across controlled simulated perturbations.

## Project Status

In development.

## Scope

This is a simulation-based research prototype developed for engineering
research and education. It is not a medical device and is not intended
for clinical use, diagnosis, treatment or surgical decision-making.
